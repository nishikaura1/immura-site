The hard part

Built to say “I don’t know”

A wrong answer from an infrastructure layer is undetectable downstream. It becomes an agent asserting a confident figure to a person who has nothing to check it against — and the longer the work runs, the further that travels before anyone notices.

So every boundary in the pipeline can decline, and prefers declining to a plausible answer. A value is read correctly or refused, never coerced. An ambiguous identity reports its candidates rather than picking one. A question the layer does not cover is answered with that fact.

The cost of refusing is measured and reported next to the accuracy, because a layer that refuses everything would be worthless — and one that never refuses is guessing.
