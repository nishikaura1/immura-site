What teams build on it

Agents that own an outcome, not a task

A single call is easy. The work that matters runs for weeks, crosses systems that disagree with each other, and has to be defensible afterwards. The systems differ by industry; the shape of the problem does not.

A quarterly scramble becomes a standing process.

Revenue

Customer resolution

One answer instead of four, with its sources.

Operations

Exception handling

Finds work stuck between systems, establishes which side is wrong, and resolves or escalates it with the reasoning attached.

Queues stop growing while people are asleep.

Finance

Findings arrive early, not in the audit.

Change management

The dependency nobody remembered gets caught.

Security

Triages inbound security requests — exceptions, escalations, tooling reviews — assembling the context from every system before the queue reaches an analyst.

Requests arrive answered, not just logged.

AI governance

Tracks which models, agents and data each team is actually using, against the policy that applies and the evidence a regulator or auditor will ask for.

The inventory is current, not a quarterly spreadsheet.
